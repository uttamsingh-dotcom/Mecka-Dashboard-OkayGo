Live data needs one more setting

This page reads the trackers straight from Google Sheets once a Google Client ID is added. Open index.html in your GitHub repo, click the pencil (Edit) icon, and paste the Client ID between the quotes of googleClientId:"" in the SETTINGS block near the top. Then click Commit changes and reload this page.

The Client ID comes from Google Cloud (APIs & Services → Credentials → OAuth client ID, type Web application). It ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com.

Sign in to see live data

This dashboard reads the Domestic and Commercial trackers straight from Google Sheets. Sign in with your BetterPlace Google account. You'll only see data your account can already open.

Nothing is stored on this site. Google keeps you signed in for about an hour at a time; after that the page asks again.

Load this month's trackers

    Notes and data checks

    How each figure is worked out, and where the tracker's own numbers disagree.

    Data checks

    Run on the latest data for the selected category.

      Definitions

      Hours collected
      Domestic counts recorded hours against target; Commercial counts uploaded hours, matching each tracker's "Achieved" figure.
      Target due to date
      The sum of daily targets up to the latest day with data. Pace compares hours collected with it.
      Pace status
      On track at 100% of the target due or more, at risk from 90%, behind under 90%.
      Approval and rejection rate
      Approved or rejected hours divided by reviewed hours (approved + rejected), the same way for both categories. Hours still waiting for QA are left out.
      Not reviewed yet
      Uploaded hours minus approved and rejected hours. It covers pending, deferred and unprocessed hours.
      Revenue and projection
      Taken as-is from the MTD Revenue MIS summary. Revenue per approved hour is revenue divided by approved hours.
      Gross margin
      Vendor revenue minus vendor payout, divided by vendor revenue, from the MTD Vendor MIS tab. Shown only when payouts are filled in.
      Rejection rate bands
      Under 25% is fine, 25–35% needs watching, 35% and above is high. These bands are a suggested starting point.
